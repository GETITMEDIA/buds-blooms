About Us

A Vision Born from Care

Since 2012, Buds & Blooms is the major retailer of Just born, Maternity and Kids products in Pondicherry. We focus on quality, a large choice, affordable prices, and convenience. Within a short period of inception, our store has been fortuned to be trusted as one-stop-shop and have a strong footing in the minds of people with its quality offer and gentle customer service.

Our store houses most popular and prestigious brands in Child care and Maternity. Shopping at Buds & Blooms is a whole new experience as it gives you perfect products with consistent and reliable quality with more value for money.
